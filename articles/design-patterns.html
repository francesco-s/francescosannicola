Design patterns describe reusable approaches to recurring software design problems. The Gang of Four catalog organizes them around three concerns: how objects are created, how they are composed, and how they collaborate.

Explore the category that matches your design problem. Each guide connects the intent of a pattern to practical examples, implementation trade-offs, and situations where it is useful.

How should objects be created?

Explore object creation and lifecycle decisions, from selecting an implementation to assembling, copying, or sharing an instance.
